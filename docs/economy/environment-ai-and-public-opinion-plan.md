# Environment priorities and pollution accountability

Draft for discussion, 2026-11-16. Baseline is Terra Invicta 1.0.65 with the installed Economic Equalization Overhaul 0.11.14 environment and emissions settings. The initial AI scope is nations with four to six actual control points (CPs).

## When the AI assigns a pip today

`AIDailyFactionPlanner.ManageNations` normally revisits each AI faction's countries twice a month on faction-specific offsets. It estimates its share of national monthly base investment points from the fraction of CPs it holds. It uses that estimate to budget **priority weights** and typically applies the same resulting weights to each CP it owns. The ordinary Environment branch has no 4–6 CP cutoff, no emissions test and no temperature test. A larger country can still crowd Environment out through military, stability, space and resource demands.

| Faction | Native `protectHumanLife` across its three objective configurations | June 2044 active value | June 2044 large nations with a pip / held | June 2044 CPs with a pip / held |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Resistance | 1 / 1 / 1 | 1 | 0 / 6 | 0 / 17 |
| Humanity First | 0.50 / 0.25 / 0.25 | 0.50 | 0 / 3 | 0 / 6 |
| Initiative | 0.50 / 0.50 / 0.45 | 0.50 | 3 / 15 | 10 / 47 |
| Servants | 0.50 / 0.40 / 0.40 | 0.50 | 1 / 3 | 1 / 9 |
| Protectorate | 1 / 0.95 / 0.45 | 1 | 1 / 3 | 1 / 5 |
| Academy | 0.85 / 0.90 / 0.85 | 0.85 | 0 / 1 | 0 / 2 |
| Project Exodus (player) | 0.85 / 0.90 / 0.85 | 0.85 | 4 / 4 | 5 / 14 |
| Aliens | Default preset path | 0.25 | 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 |

- **At 1 or higher**, the ordinary management branch goes through general problem, research and priority-budget checks. It may assign Government first. If the total desired weights are still at most six, it requests one to three Environment pips from the remaining budget estimate, with a minimum request of one.
- **Below 1**, the ordinary branch requires zero counted problems and democracy below `8 + protectHumanLife`. After Government and Funding, the AI requests one Environment pip per three whole remaining budget units, up to three. At the June settings, the democracy cutoff is 8.85 for the Academy and AI Exodus, and 8.5 for Humanity First, the Initiative and the Servants. The Protectorate changes branches when its objective configuration drops below 1.
- **Overrides and final allocation.** Capital-defense, active-war and the separate major-unrest branch skip this ordinary calculation. Pillage and Neutralize goals can replace the ordinary allocation. The game can add weights from goals and spare-budget presets, but every faction default preset has zero Environment, including the Alien preset. An urgent hab-objective Mission Control override can subtract one from other desired weights. Priority bonuses, rescaling and stochastic rounding can still erase a one-pip request before the game writes the CPs.
- **Eligibility.** A selected weight only buys investment while Environment is valid. The mod checks the research ceiling through the native validity path, and allows decontamination where applicable. At the absolute rating of 10, Environment can perform atmospheric removal while persistent greenhouse concentrations remain above the reference. A stored pip can be dormant when the priority is unavailable.

The June snapshot has 23 large nations, all eligible for Environment, and seven with any live Environment pip. Three had at least one pip from an AI. A mixed nation appears under each faction that holds a CP there. At earlier December 2042 and November 2043 checkpoints the Resistance allocated Environment in one of six large holdings. It allocated zero across five sampled dates from February to June 2044. The Initiative ranged from one to three of 14–15 holdings across the seven samples. The Protectorate funded one holding in every sample. The Academy and Servants moved between zero and one, and Humanity First remained at zero. These are snapshots of one campaign and do not identify which native condition caused a particular zero.

## Where the climate cost lands

The June 16 save reconstructs to **+2.705 °C** with the Environment technology ceiling at **4**. The rounding below uses the installed mod's emissions formulas with saved GDP, region populations and stored sustainability. All-gas figures translate the game’s CO₂, methane and nitrous oxide inputs into one CO₂-equivalent unit using the game’s temperature weights and tons-to-ppm conversions. They are estimated annual routine rates, separate from Spoils bursts, atmospheric sinks and changes in economic activity.

|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| India | Exodus | 3.25 | 6.97 | 5.6% | 0.37 |
| China | Servants | 2.96 | 5.00 | 1.9% from Exodus | 0.41 |
| Brazil | Resistance | 0.88 | 3.58 | 0% | 2.23 |
| Indonesia | Resistance | 1.11 | 3.31 | 0% | 1.80 |
| Saudi Arabia | Initiative | 0.53 | 2.85 | 0% | 2.34 |
| European Union | Resistance | 1.79 | 2.28 | 0% | 0.98 |
| United Kingdom | Initiative | 3.25 | 0.24 | 16.7% | 0.02 |

The allocation column averages each CP's valid Environment weight divided by its saved total weight, before priority bonuses. The last column models a year of Environment IP at 10% of pre-upkeep IP output with the installed income ramp and no project bonuses or fallout. It reports the reduction in the annual run rate **at the end of the year**. It is an order-of-magnitude comparison, with GDP, population and technology held fixed. Brazil, Indonesia and the EU were all at peace in the snapshot and together produced about 14% of estimated global routine warming-equivalent emissions. Vietnam adds another Resistance executive example outside the four-CP cutoff, with zero Environment and roughly 1.13 Gt/yr. India leads the total but most of its current emissions are methane and nitrous oxide in the model. At cap 4 the steep CO₂ curve makes investment in Brazil and Indonesia much more effective over the next year.

The native monthly climate update collects emissions and applies annualized GDP damage to extant nations according to common warming, local population-weighted exposure and inequality. At +2.705 °C, inequality around 3.4–4.5 and exposure 1.0, the installed multiplier produces about 2.3–2.7% annual GDP damage. Actual exposure varies. Environment completion improves sustainability, performs removal where valid, or advances fallout cleanup. It has no recurring public-opinion effect tied to emissions. The warming narrative events can produce public-opinion effects from individual event choices. Unity already changes opinion, and Spoils can add pollution and carries an education/democracy opinion penalty. These effects do not apportion climate accountability to the factions governing a major emitter. [Official climate reference](https://wiki.hoodedhorse.com/Terra_Invicta/Climate_Change)

## Proposed AI behavior

1. **Fund the most useful cleanup the faction can actually deliver.** Reuse the mod's emissions and advancement math. Estimate how much the annual emissions rate would fall if the faction put a modest share of its own usable IP into Environment over the next year. Account for the current research ceiling and how much of the country the faction controls. Use absolute benefit to rank countries, with continuous warming pressure and a tunable faction preference. Give a significant emitter with affordable cuts at least one actual pip on enough eligible owned CPs to matter. Start balance comparisons around 5–10% of national IP in normal cases under majority control and 10–15% for the few highest-payoff cases in a severe climate. A minority controller contributes from its own CPs. Brazil, Indonesia and the EU should be early Resistance candidates in the June save. Saudi Arabia should register as a high-payoff cleanup case even for the Initiative. India should stay eligible and gain urgency as better mitigation becomes possible.
2. **Spend from the ordinary national plan.** Let Environment compete with discretionary growth, surplus Funding and optional projects. Keep the immediate defense of a threatened capital and emergency stabilization ahead of the allocation. Use the defensive situation, so a country in a quiet or stalemated war can still invest. Keep legitimate wartime tradeoffs and show when they explain a skipped allocation. Once the priority qualifies, check the final pip after normal rounding. Use the same active-CP validity and normal action path as other AI priorities. At a temporary technology ceiling, only budget for cleanup the priority can actually perform, with dedicated consideration for rating-10 atmospheric removal or genuine fallout. Use a little hysteresis around the cutoff so a country does not toggle each review.
3. **Give factions distinct thresholds in climate-specific data.** The Resistance should start earliest and sustain cleanup through ordinary competition for IP. The Academy should be close behind and favor large, achievable reductions. The Protectorate can be proactive early, with weaker commitment as its agenda changes. Humanity First should generally fund cleanup in secure countries and put more weight on immediate defense. The Initiative should respond strongly to a visible political cost, especially where cleanup is cheap or Spoils is increasing damage. Exodus should respond to the threat to its economic base, with launch and infrastructure demands competing for the same budget. The Servants should start with a weak preference and respond to severe damage or local pressure. The Alien Administration uses a separate native path and merits its own decision about human opposition and the game's alien-opinion mapping. Changing the existing faction presets or broad `protectHumanLife` values would also change unrelated AI priorities and would still lack an emissions signal.

## Proposed public reaction

- **Domestic accountability.** Review once a month. Compute pressure from sustained avoidable emissions with two inputs, a material contribution to global warming and excess emissions relative to a reachable benchmark for the country's economy and technology. Use the same emissions math for both. A fixed normalization scale and smooth curve let an absolute cut improve the standing of a polluter even if the rest of the world cleans up faster. Recent Spoils should aggravate the source footprint once measurable, with actual Spoils investment or payouts informing attribution. Let the pressure recede as emissions fall. A zero-to-full warming response from roughly +1.5 to +2.75 °C is a starting curve to test.
- **Assign responsibility to the people who can act.** A first test could put half the accountability weight on executive control and half on effective CP share. Split the latter by faction and leave native-owned share unattributed. This gives the executive a real cost while limiting the cost to a single minority CP holder. Use actual current control over time so new owners have a transition period and repeated transfers do not erase the country's emissions record. Show recent improvement as relief in the forecast. Apply the same public rule to human factions regardless of ideology, including the player. Faction values govern the AI response.
- **Make the penalty visible and bounded.** Start with up to about 0.25 percentage points of monthly domestic support loss at full responsibility and maximum pressure. Scale the realized shift down when existing support is small and transfer it to Undecided. Compare a deterministic transfer with the native negative propaganda path. The native operation uses stochastic ideology-neighbor redistribution, and the owned-CP-fraction convenience method integer-divides, so a piecemeal use of either would not deliver predictable attribution in split nations. Any new opinion mutation should preserve normalization and follow the game's Influence cache and nation dirty-state updates. Sample the monthly effect once, with a clear source breakdown, to avoid both spamming and stacking every polluter on every country.
- **Small international cost.** The preferred first comparison includes backlash in other countries, scaled by a faction's aggregate responsibility for major polluters. Test a combined foreign cap around 0.05 percentage points per month per faction per affected country. Domestic cases already charged should avoid a duplicate charge from the same source. Compare with domestic-only pressure if the international effect becomes hard to read or distorts Influence. An alternative is to let credible evidence of major pollution strengthen hostile Public Campaigns, creating an active player response at the cost of councilor time.
- **Offer a political route to actual cleanup.** Native rules already allow Environment Direct Investment if the faction owns a CP, or if the nation still has a native CP and open borders, subject to valid priority and the annual IP cap. Fully faction-held outsiders need a different route. Test whether visible domestic pressure and the AI response are enough first. If the player still needs an intervention, consider a negotiated mitigation commitment or targeted foreign Environment investment with executive consent. Evaluate this against a fully held polluter where an outsider currently has no investment access.

## Next balance pass

Compare native and proposed allocations for the same 2044 positions, then run parallel timelines from the same save when implementation is approved. Record final pips, national IP share, rating and emissions with and without policy, monthly support and Influence, and diversion from emergency defense or space bootstrap. Include an early-start checkpoint, a split government, a cap-bound large emitter, a recently captured polluter, a rating-10 remover and an active capital-defense case. The first design decisions are whether the international response should be passive or campaign-driven, how far the Resistance and Academy should lead the other factions, and how much domestic opinion should move over a year of sustained pollution.
