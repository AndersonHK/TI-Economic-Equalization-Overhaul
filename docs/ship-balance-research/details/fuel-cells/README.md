# Fuel-cell research

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

## Current synthesis

The game's fuel cells are localized as alkaline hydrogen/oxygen systems
recharged by solar arrays. Their balance must therefore include more than a
fuel-cell stack: solar collection, electrolysis, reactant storage, water
handling, conditioning, and local thermal interfaces all belong to the
described power plant. Radiator panels remain separate ship modules;
efficiency determines their required capacity but not power-plant mass.

The [catalysis, specific-power, and thermal analysis](catalysis-power-density-and-thermal-limits.md)
finds:

- the settled planning efficiencies of `58/60/62%` are generous but
  thermodynamically possible on discharge;
- after the later doubling, Fuel Cell III's settled `0.96 kg/kW` gives
  `1.04 kW/kg`, about half a DOE `2 kW/kg` stack-only target, despite the game
  plant also accounting for solar collection and regenerative hardware;
- at roughly `90°C`, corrected waste heat requires about `691–816 m²` of
  ideal radiator per electric megawatt;
- a catalyst can reduce overpotential but cannot remove reactant transport,
  electrolyte, water, current collection, or the separate radiator burden;
- engineered hydrogenases could improve selectivity, oxygen or poison
  tolerance, and precious-metal use, but current evidence does not support a
  generic power-per-mass advantage over synthetic stacks.

The strongest physical constraint on gigawatt alkaline fuel cells is
low-temperature heat rejection, not the ideal H2/O2 energy balance.
